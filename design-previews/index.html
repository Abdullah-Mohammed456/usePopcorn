What the reference research contributes

Netflix organizes discovery into recommended and genre rows, with shortcuts and popular lists. IMDb adds searchable catalog patterns, ratings, and sortable watchlists. MUBI gives collections an editorial point of view. Letterboxd makes ratings and a personal film diary central. JustWatch’s provider filters are useful research, but outside this app’s current feature set.
